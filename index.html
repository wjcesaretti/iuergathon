Who We Are

Built to Compete

IU Rowing Club is a nationally competitive, student-run and student-funded organization committed to introducing the students of Indiana University to the sport of rowing, as well as developing them as people on and off of the water.

We as a club have been around since the 1980s, with our most recent reestablishment bringing rowing and its benefits back to campus in 2023. Back then, the club's reiteration began only with a handful of students with hardly any experience and even less rowing equipment. Since then, we as a club have grown rapidly thanks in large part to the support of our friends, family, and the community surrounding us. Today, we are comprised of over 35 highly dedicated athletes that train six days a week and travel across the country to represent Indiana University.

This spring, we're bringing that intensity to the water and building toward competing at the national level. The standard at IU has never been higher and we intend to meet it.

Why We Need You

Your Support Fuels the Drive

As a club sport, the team receives minimal direct funding from the university and is almost exclusively funded through athlete dues and fundraising. Rowing is traditionally an expensive sport and we are trying to keep it accessible to the IU community so that our boat selection can be based on athlete merit rather than purely financial means.

This spring, we're raising $10,000 to fuel our racing season. Every dollar goes directly to our athletes and our program.
