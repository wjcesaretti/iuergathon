Who We Are

Built From Scratch

IU Rowing Club is a competitive, student-run and student-funded organization at Indiana University, committed to bringing the sport of rowing to Bloomington and developing our athletes on and off the water.

We refounded the club in 2023 — starting with a small group of students, hardly any experience, and even less equipment. Since then, we've grown into a dedicated team that trains six days a week and competes at regattas across the region. Our goal is to keep the sport accessible to any IU student who wants to row, while building a program that can compete at the national level.

Why We Need You

Your Support Makes Our Season

As a student organization, we receive very little funding from the university. Our season runs on member dues, fundraising, and support from our friends, family, and community. The costs of competing — boats, oars, safety launches, fuel, travel, regatta fees — add up fast.

We work hard to keep dues as affordable as possible so that no student is priced out of the sport. That means community support — your support — is what makes the difference between a team that trains and a team that competes.

This spring, we're raising $7,500 to fund our racing season. Every dollar goes directly to our athletes and our program. We appreciate any help you can provide.
